[Rebuild all three reels and re-score tactics](../docs/reproducibility.md) from a clean checkout. New encodes are equivalents, not exact-byte copies. The separate v0.1.1 narration bundle contains original base/technical voice takes and regenerated coaching takes, not a base MP4.

The [clean-checkout record](../docs/reproduction-check.md) links the executed commands and [new verification receipts](reproduction/). It covers all three editions, a fresh technical voice synthesis, and deliberately altered videos that the checks rejected.

This is a match-specific rebuild, not an automatic new-video commentary workflow. The moments, player names, shot terms, title, and lines were edited for this match. A separate fresh clip-04 technique-short export and recheck passed. The chapter-based editorial exporter also exported public recut clip 04, but passes or is rejected depending on local voice length against its 63-frame legs budget: seven of ten fresh voice processes fit, three exceeded it. Re-run after a speech-fit rejection. The guard is unchanged; a longer-window fix is not implemented. Fresh-voice MP4 hashes identify individual runs, not expected regenerated bytes. See the [TikTok-style workflow status](../docs/tiktok-style-workflow.md) and [command receipt](reproduction/any-clip-commands.json).
