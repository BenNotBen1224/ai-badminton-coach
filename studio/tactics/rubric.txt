WHY / WHAT-NEXT RUBRIC for narration scripts

Unit: one spoken sentence (split at 。！？ and at line ends). Count every sentence, including the
opening and the closing summary. The denominator is all sentences.

Give each sentence exactly one class:
  WHY    gives a reason for a hitter's choice that names the OTHER player's state: where she is,
         which way she is moving, how far she is from her base, whether she is balanced, or how
         much time she has. Both parts must be there: the opponent's state and the choice.
         Example: "她还在后场没回中，所以这拍吊网前。"
  NEXT   says what should come next because of this shot: the likely reply, where the receiver
         will have to go, or where the hitter must move now. Example: "她在网前低点接球，要准备
         她挑高，也要防她回网。" Possible replies are not inevitable replies.
  CHECK  says whether that expected next thing happened. Example: "果然，她挑了后场。"
  SEE    describes body or footwork only (what a joint, foot or arm does).
  TIP    general advice that would fit any rally ("每一步都要回中").
  OTHER  hook, score, link words, anything else.

Evidence tie (needed for WHY, NEXT and CHECK to count):
  the line carries evidence ids that point to measured entries in analysis.json (positions,
  distance from base, movement direction or speed, time to next hit, next contact spot), AND the
  screen shows that evidence while the sentence plays (mini-court dot, arrow, open-space patch,
  next-receiver-contact patch, base marker). A WHY/NEXT/CHECK without both does not count.
  A receiver's next contact position is not the shuttle's landing position.

Score = (tied WHY + tied NEXT + tied CHECK) / all sentences.
Target for the new video: score >= 0.60, and no sentence claims more than the data shows
(no tracked shuttle flight, no 3D, no speed of the shuttle, no player intent stated as fact:
intent is said as a reading, e.g. "像是想…", "这一拍的意思是…").
