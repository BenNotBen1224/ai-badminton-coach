Confirmed requirements
 1. Run real pose on the main-camera stretches of the whole reel (30 fps is fine) with the
    existing pipeline; keep the per-frame pose cache in the output folder.
 2. Split rallies: score states + pose jump cuts + serve start. Give each rally its score, game,
    start and end time.
 3. Find hits (every racket contact, serve included), each with: time, hitter (near/far and
    which player), and a confidence. You may use the arena sound: the reel keeps court audio and
    racket hits are sharp clicks (a quick test with a first-difference RMS over 20 ms windows,
    peaks above 4x median, gave clicks about once a second in 268-296 s). Combine sound with
    pose (swing, wrist speed, who is reaching) and with alternation (hitters alternate).
 4. Per hit, record in court metres (CourtGeometry / foot_court): both players' foot positions at
    contact; the opponent's movement direction and speed over the 0.2 s before contact; each
    player's distance from her own base (base = her usual waiting spot in this match, e.g. median
    position at the opponent's contacts; say how you define it); a simple balance state
    (settled / moving / stretched-lunge / airborne) from pose; contact height (overhead / side /
    underarm from wrist vs head and hip); time to the next contact; the receiver's foot position
    at the next contact.
 5. Inferred landing zone = the receiver's next contact spot as a 3x3 zone on her half (front /
    mid / rear with front = net to short service line, rear = last third; left / center / right as
    thirds of the singles width as seen on screen). Last hit of a rally: "unknown" unless you can
    support it. Always label this as inferred from the next contact, never as a tracked flight.
 6. Inferred shot family (serve, clear, lift, smash, drop, net, drive/push, block, unknown) from
    contact height, where the hitter and receiver are, and time to next contact. Keep the rules
    simple and written down; low-evidence cases must say unknown.
 7. Two measured "feedback loop" numbers per hit, both from data you already have:
      pull_m  = distance from where the receiver stood at this contact to where she hit next
      against = whether that move went against her movement direction at contact (wrong foot)
    These are what the later narration will point at.
 8. Behaviour tests with literal expected values for the inference rules (synthetic positions in,
    exact family/zone out), plus the existing suite where it applies.
 9. Write a fresh folder ROOT\badminton-coach\outputs\tactics-engine-20261104\ with hits.json
    (one row per hit with all fields above and evidence frame numbers), rallies.json, the pose
    cache, run.json, tests.json and handoff.json (absolute paths, changed source files with hashes,
    counts, honest limits). Do not touch older outputs. Use the CPU lock for heavy runs:
    ROOT\obj2\work\cpu.lock via audit\with_lock.py.
10. Report back: counts (rallies, hits, hits per rally), how hits were found, rule tables for
    zone/family, run time, test results, and every limit (2D only, no shuttle tracking, etc.).
